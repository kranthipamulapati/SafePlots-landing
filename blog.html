Why NRI Plots are Easy Targets

NRI (Non-Resident Indian) properties, particularly vacant plots, are high-stakes targets for encroachment and land-grabbing schemes in rapidly developing cities like Hyderabad. The reasons are multifaceted, but the primary factor is absence.

When a property remains unmonitored for months or years, it signals to local land sharks that the owner is likely unaware of ground realities. Encroachers often use sophisticated tactics:

  • Temporary Structures: Starting with a small hut or garbage dumping to test the waters.
  • Forged Documents: Creating 'mirror' documents to claim ownership in local courts.
  • Boundary Shifting: Gradually moving fences of adjacent properties to slowly consume yours.

SafePlots bridges this geographical gap using high-resolution satellite imagery and frequent ground verification, ensuring that your property is never truly "absent."

Understanding Vacant Land Tax (VLT)

Vacant Land Tax (VLT) is a mandatory tax levied by the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC) and other municipalities across Telangana on unconstructed plots. In 2026, with the merger of several peripheral municipalities into GHMC, the enforcement is stricter than ever.

How is VLT calculated?
In Hyderabad, VLT is typically charged at 0.5% of the estimated capital value of the land. If the land is not maintained (e.g., used for illegal dumping), a penalty of 0.25% can be added.

Why you shouldn't ignore it:
Accumulating VLT dues can lead to:

  • High interest penalties (often 24% per annum).
  • Difficulties during property sale or registration.
  • Legal notices from the municipal authorities.

SafePlots helps NRIs track their VLT status and facilitates timely payments through our unified dashboard, preventing costly legal headaches.

Precast Walls vs Fencing

Choosing the right physical barrier for your plot is the first line of defense. But should you go for a simple barbed wire fence or a robust precast compound wall?

Barbed Wire Fencing:
Pros: Cost-effective, quick to install (usually 1 day), clear visual marker of ownership.
Cons: Easily cut, provides minimal physical protection against animals or persistent trespassers.

Precast Compound Walls:
Pros: Extremely durable, provides high privacy, very difficult to breach, increases property value.
Cons: Higher initial investment, requires 2-3 days for installation.

At SafePlots, we recommend Precast walls for plots in rapidly developing areas where encroachment risks are higher. For remote layouts, a high-quality ID board paired with barbed wire is a great starting point.
