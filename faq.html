FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about SafePlots services, pricing, and how we protect your land.

HOW DOES SATELLITE MONITORING WORK? ▼
We task high-resolution satellites to revisit your plot every 5 days. We use this imagery to perform checks and detect any changes on the ground (like new structures, debris, or clearing). If we detect anomalies, we alert you immediately.
CAN I MONITOR MULTIPLE PLOTS? ▼
Yes! Our "Unified Dashboard" allows you to add and manage as many plots as you own. You can see the status of all your properties in one single view. We also offer custom pricing for multi-plot owners.
WHAT CITIES DO YOU COVER? ▼
Currently, we focus on Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Guntur, and Vizag. We are rapidly expanding to other major cities in India. If you have a plot elsewhere, contact us, and we might still be able to help with satellite monitoring.
DO YOU PROVIDE LEGAL ASSISTANCE? ▼
Yes, we monitor Encumbrance Certificates (EC) and Vacant Land Tax (VLT) status. If legal issues arise, we can connect you with trusted property lawyers in our network, but we are primarily a monitoring and management service.
HOW OFTEN ARE PHYSICAL VISITS CONDUCTED? ▼
It depends on your plan. "Digital Shield" is purely digital (satellite + records). "Protection Pro" includes 2 scheduled physical visits per year. You can also request on-demand "One-Time Visits" whenever you like for an additional fee.
