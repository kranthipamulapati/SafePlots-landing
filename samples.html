ID Board Installation

We install high-visibility ID boards on your plot. These rugged, weather-proof signage boards deter land grabbers & trespassers.

  • ✓ Legal Disclaimers: legal warnings can be included on request to deter potential encroachers.
  • ✓ Ownership Records: Official ownership records can be included on request for added authenticity.

Professional Fencing

Secure your plot boundaries with our professional fencing solutions. From barbed wire to precast compound walls, we handle complete installation.

  • ✓ Multiple Options: Barbed wire or precast compound walls.
  • ✓ Quality Materials: Durable construction for long-lasting protection.
  • ✓ End-to-End Service: We manage everything from survey, labour, material procurement, transport & installation.

Dharani data

Hyderabad Lakes FTL & Buffer Zones

We verify if your plot falls within lake boundaries or Full Tank Level (FTL) zones. This critical check prevents future legal complications.

  • ✓ Coverage: 1000+ lakes in & around Hyderabad.
  • ✓ FTL & buffer zones: Check against HMDA/GHMC lake FTL & buffer zones.
  • ✓ Legal Clarity: Avoid construction restrictions & demolition risks.

Satellite Monitoring

We use high-resolution satellite imagery to track your plot every 5 days. Our advanced NDVI analysis detects vegetation changes and potential encroachments automatically.

  • ✓ Before & After: Compare imagery over time to spot changes.
  • ✓ Resolution: High-res imagery clear enough to see new structures.
  • ✓ Frequency: Revisit time of 5 days ensures rapid detection.

Comprehensive Reports

Receive detailed monthly PDF reports that summarize all monitoring activities. Perfect for your records and peace of mind.

  • ✓ Status Summary: Clear safe/unsafe status indicators.
