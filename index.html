HOW IT WORKS?

We combine technology & on-ground efforts to offer comprehensive protection for your plot.

ONLINE MONITORING

  • 🛰️
    SATELLITE (REMOTE SENSING)
    We collect data from high resolution satellites & perform NDVI analysis to detect encroachments. Revisit time is 5 days.
  • 📝
    REGISTRATION (EC) CHECKS
    We check for registration fraud by performing weekly encumbrance certificate (EC) verification.
  • 🧾
    VACANT LAND TAX
    We check for vacant land tax dues & send notifications to ensure tax compliance.

OFFLINE SERVICES

  • 🧱
    PHYSICAL FENCING
    Robust compound walls or barbed wire to secure boundaries.
  • 🪧
    ID BOARD INSTALLATION
    Clear ownership signage to deter illegal land grabbers.
  • 📍
    PHYSICAL SITE VISITS
    Regular ground inspections with photo/video proof.
