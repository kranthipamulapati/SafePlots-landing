Monitoring

We monitor your plots & provide you with the latest updates.

  • 📝 REGISTRATION (EC)
  • 🛰️ SATELLITE (REMOTE SENSING)
  • 🧾 VACANT LAND TAX
  • 🌐 GOOGLE EARTH
REGISTRATION
  • Frequency: Weekly
  • Checks for changes in Encumbrance Certificate
  • Helps in detection of registration fraud
SATELLITE
  • Frequency: 5 days
  • Remote sensing using satellite imagery
  • Normalised Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) analysis
  • Helps in detection of encroachments
VACANT LAND TAX
  • Frequency: 15 days
  • Checks for due payment of vacant land tax
  • Reminders for non-payment of vacant land tax
GOOGLE EARTH
  • Frequency: 15 days
  • Checks for changes in image acquisition date in google earth

SERVICES

🗺️

FINDER

Lost track of your plot location? We help you physically locate your plot using survey maps and ground teams.

📍

VISIT

Cannot visit in person? We provide physical site inspections, giving you a complete on-ground perspective.

🚧

FENCING

Secure your boundaries effectively. We install robust Precast Compound Walls or Barbed Wire Fencing to protect your asset from encroachment.

🧹

CLEANING

Keep your plot well-maintained. We offer regular clearing of wild vegetation and debris to keep your property clean and accessible.

ID BOARD

Establish ownership clearly. We fabricate and install durable ID boards on your plot, displaying ownership details to deter illegal claims.
