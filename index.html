HOW IT WORKS?

01
📂
Data Collection
We collect initial data which may include EC, VLT, coordinates, layout info & collect additional data by doing a thorough search online (government & layout developer websites etc) .
02
📍
Initial Visit & Assessment
Our team will physically locate the plot, identify boundaries & assess conditio. We will send you a comprehensive report with geotagged photos.
03
🤝
Onboarding
Your plot is added to our platform where you can view all details & track progress. We setup monitoring & services schedules based on your requirements.
04
🛰️
Satellite Monitoring
We task high-resolution satellites to track your plot. Re-visit time is 5 days & we use data from satellites to perform NDVI analysis to detect any changes.
05
📝
Registration Monitoring
We monitor Encumbrance Certificates (EC) weekly to detect & notify you of any changes.
06
🧾
Tax Monitoring
We track Vacant Land Tax dues & send timely notifications to ensure full tax compliance.
07
🪧
ID Board Installation
Placement of a clear ownership signboard on-site to assert possession & deter encroachers.
08
🧱
Fencing
Installation of physical barriers like compound walls or barbed wire as per your specific requirements.
09
📅
Scheduled Inspections
Regular physical visits (Quarterly/Half-yearly) with detailed PDF reports & fresh visual evidence.
10
🚨
Risk Alerts
Instant notifications if any risk is detected via satellite data, ground visits, or legal checks.
