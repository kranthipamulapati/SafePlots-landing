Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Overview

At SafePlots, we strive to provide the best plot management services. This policy outlines our approach to refunds and cancellations for both subscription services and one-time projects.

2. Subscription Cancellation

2.1 Monthly Subscriptions

  • You may cancel your monthly subscription at any time.
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle.
  • No refund is provided for the current billing period.
  • You will continue to receive services until the end of the paid period.
  • To cancel, contact us at least 7 days before your next billing date.

2.2 Annual Subscriptions

  • Annual plans can be cancelled within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
  • After 30 days, refunds are prorated based on remaining months.
  • Refund processing takes 7-10 business days.
  • Administrative fee of ₹500 may apply for mid-term cancellations.

3. One-Time Service Cancellation

3.1 Before Work Begins

  • Full refund available if cancelled at least 48 hours before scheduled start.
  • 50% refund if cancelled within 24-48 hours before start.
  • No refund for cancellations less than 24 hours before start.

3.2 After Work Has Started

No refund once work has commenced. Payment for work completed up to cancellation point is required. Materials purchased specifically for your project are non-refundable.

4. Refund Eligibility

You may be eligible for a full or partial refund if:

  • Scheduled visits were not completed as per your plan
  • Services provided were significantly different from what was promised
  • Technical issues prevented dashboard access for >7 days
  • We were unable to access your property despite proper notice

5. Non-Refundable Items

Setup fees, Drone survey fees (once conducted), Custom project materials, and Third-party fees are non-refundable.

6. Suspension vs Cancellation

Temporary Suspension: You may suspend for up to 3 months without cancellation (no charges, no visits).
Permanent Cancellation: Terminates all services and access.

7. How to Request Refund

  1. Contact us at admin@safeplots.com
  2. Provide account details and reason
  3. Include supporting docs

Review: 3-5 days. Processing: 7-10 days.

8. Force Majeure

No refunds for interruptions due to natural disasters or government restrictions. We will extend your period instead.
