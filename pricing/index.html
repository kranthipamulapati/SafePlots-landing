Why fence the plot?

  • Deter encroachment: Fencing creates a clear physical boundary, helps deter encroachment from neighboring plots. Unfortunately, encroachment by neighboring plots is a common problem in India.
  • Increase property value: Fenced plots are easier to sell & sell at a premium price compared to unfenced plots. It’s a one-time investment that can be recouped through increased plot value.

Why vacant land tax is a must?

  • Establish ownership: VLT creates a paper/digital trail proving your ownership of the plot & helps in case of legal disputes with encroachers.
  • Saves money: Many plot owners construct a room & take electricity connection. This is not required if the plot is fenced & VLT is paid. Saving money on construction & electricity connection.
  • Legal compliance: Vacant land tax is a legal requirement in Telangana.
